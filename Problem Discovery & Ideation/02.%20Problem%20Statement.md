## Step 1: Problem Identification

Students often depend on separate resources for questions, explanations, quizzes, notes, summaries and learning direction. This creates a fragmented study workflow.

## Step 2: Problem Statements

| S.No | Problem Statement | User Impact | Priority |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Students need one place to ask questions across multiple learning topics. | Time is spent switching between tools. | High |
| 2 | Difficult topics are not always available in a beginner-friendly explanation. | Students may struggle to understand key concepts. | High |
| 3 | Students need quick practice and revision material. | Revision and practice may become inconsistent. | High |
| 4 | Students may not know what to learn next. | Learning can become unstructured. | Medium |

## Step 3: Final Problem Statement

> How might we provide students with a single AI-powered learning assistant that can answer questions, explain concepts, generate quizzes and study notes, summarize content, and recommend a structured learning path in a simple interface?
